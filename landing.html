About This Portal

This portal allows SIH 2025 leaders to register their teams and manage their participation in the event.

Features

  • Secure authentication with email verification
  • Team registration with member details
  • Dashboard to view announcements and resources
  • Admin panel for managing teams, announcements, and resources

Getting Started

To get started, simply sign up for an account using the link below. After verifying your email, you'll be able to register your team.

Login / Sign Up

User Roles

Leader

  • Signs up with email + password
  • Registers team details
  • Views announcements and resources
  • Edits team information

Admin

  • Manages announcements and resources
  • Views all registered teams
  • Creates and deletes content
